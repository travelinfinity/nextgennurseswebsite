What is the difference between the Online Module and the Face-to-Face Intensive?

The AHPRA RN OSCE Online Module (A$218.90 including GST) is the online app you study at your own pace. The Face-to-Face Intensive (A$1,950) includes that same online module, plus a 1-week in-person course in a hospital clinical simulation lab where an OSCE trainer observes you closely as you perform the stations.

Why must the intensive be 2–3 weeks before my exam?

It leaves enough time to fix the feedback from your trainer while the hands-on practice is still fresh, without leaving it so long that you lose the rhythm of the stations.

Why are face-to-face places limited?

This is an intensive prep that needs close observation by the OSCE trainer. Keeping each intake to 8–10 students means everyone gets hands-on turns and individual feedback.

How do I pay for the face-to-face intensive?
